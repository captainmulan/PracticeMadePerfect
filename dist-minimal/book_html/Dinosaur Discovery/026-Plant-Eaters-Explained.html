Deep dive · Chewing Machines

📖 Chewing Machines

Hadrosaurs with dental batteries ground plants like living mills. Kai counted hundreds of teeth in a jaw cast. "Replace, chew, repeat — factories of foliage."
💡 Explanation: Hadrosaurs had rows of stacked teeth for grinding tough plants. Some had crests for sound. They were abundant Cretaceous herbivores.
Deep dive · Moving Meadows

📖 Moving Meadows

Drone footage of migrating wildebeest reminded Kai of sauropod herds in books — clouds of dust, babies in the middle. "Plants regrow when grazers move," Dr. Mara said.
💡 Explanation: Trackways and bone beds suggest herding in sauropods and ornithopods. Groups reduced predation risk and helped find fresh feeding grounds.
Deep dive · Forest Feast

📖 Forest Feast

Kai sampled fern tea (safely, with permission) and grimaced. "Low quality food means long guts," Dr. Mara explained. "Plant-eaters spent much of life eating — and watching for shadows."
💡 Explanation: Herbivorous dinosaurs had specialized teeth, beaks, and digestive systems. Gastroliths (swallowed stones) may have helped grind food in some species.
