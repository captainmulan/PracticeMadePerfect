Thingyan Water

During Thingyan Su splashes Water with laughing friends in the street. Drums beat and dancers spin through the bright morning. No one stays dry, and no one seems to mind. Grandmother says water washes old worries away. Su feels new and light for the year ahead.
During Thingyan Su splashes Water with laughing friends in the busy street. Drums beat and dancers spin through the bright morning sun. Bowls and hoses send cool streams over every smiling face. No one stays dry, and no one seems to mind at all. Grandmother says water washes old worries away and welcomes a fresh year. Su helps a small child fill a cup. She feels new, light, and ready for the days ahead.

Thadingyut Candles

On Thadingyut night Su carries a candle with careful hands. Lights line every path to the Pagoda on the hill. Families bow and share sweet cakes under the full moon. Su whispers thanks for the Light that guides them. The village glows like a river of gold.
On Thadingyut night Su carries a candle with careful hands beside Mother. Lights line every path to the Pagoda on the quiet hill. Families bow and share sweet cakes under the bright full moon. Uncle hangs another lantern while Aunt hums a soft song. Su whispers thanks for the Light that guides kind hearts home. Grandfather watches the glow with peaceful eyes. The whole village shines like a river of gold.

Tazaungdaing Balloons

Paper Balloons rise into the dark sky one after another. Monks walk through the village at dawn with calm steps. Su helps her brother weave a lantern for the night. The village cheers as lights climb higher and higher. Su watches until the last glow fades among the stars.
Paper Balloons rise into the dark sky one after another above the trees. Monks walk through the village at dawn with calm and gentle steps. Su helps her brother weave a lantern from bamboo and bright paper. Father lights the flame when the wind is soft. The village cheers as lights climb higher and higher into the night. Su holds Mother's hand and watches until the last glow fades among the stars. She feels part of a festival bigger than herself.
