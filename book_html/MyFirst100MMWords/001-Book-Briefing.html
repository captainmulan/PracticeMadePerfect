📖 About This Book

My First 100 Myanmar Words helps overseas kids stay connected to Myanmar through hearing and speaking — not writing drills.

Each chapter has a short story, tap-to-hear word cards, a sentences page, and a quiz. Three big games bookend the journey!

🎮 Three Hero Games

Start · Page 4 🏘️ Build Myanmar Village Stamp houses, pagodas & animals on your canvas.
Mid-book 🪔 Lantern Run Jump through Thadingyut to reach family!
Finale · Page 37 🎆 Festival Parade Collect lanterns and light up the village!

👂 How It Works

1

Pick your learner on page 3.

2

Tap 🔊 Hear on any word or sentence.

3

Repeat aloud — English on the page, Myanmar in your ears.

4

Play games and quizzes to earn badges!

This book is hear-first. It does not teach Myanmar writing or spelling.
