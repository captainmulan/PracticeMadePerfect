Why it works

The natural order

👂 Hear
👀 Recognize
🗣️ Speak
📖 Read
✍️ Write

Myanmar letters are hard before children have heard enough words. Stories and games keep them smiling while grandparents' language stays alive at home.

  • English story + moral on every chapter
  • Tap Hear on word cards — ears first
  • Sentence deep-dives & chapter quizzes
  • Overall quiz + congratulations finale

Author's message

“Hello! I'm Jimmy Cooper. Many overseas Myanmar families told me the same worry: ‘My child is forgetting our language.’ This book starts with hearing and speaking inside stories — the way village children learned under banyan trees. Three games, ten worlds, one hundred words. Writing comes later. Let's keep your child's connection to Myanmar alive, one word at a time.”
