Welcome

About this book

Welcome! This book helps children aged 4–10 learn their first Myanmar words through illustrated moral tales, tap-to-hear audio, and mini-games — without reading Myanmar script on the page.

Every chapter follows the same path: read an English story from Myanmar tradition, learn what it teaches, then tap word cards to hear Myanmar spoken aloud. Your ears learn first; writing comes later.

Author's message

"Hello! I'm Jimmy Cooper. Many overseas Myanmar families told me the same worry: 'My child is forgetting our language.' This book starts with hearing and speaking inside stories — the way village children learned under banyan trees. Writing comes later. Let's keep your child's connection to Myanmar alive, one word at a time."
