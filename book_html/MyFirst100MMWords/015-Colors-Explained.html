Gold at Dawn

The pagoda shines Gold at sunrise above the misty hills. Su wears a Red ribbon to school and feels brave. Yellow mangoes glow at the market beside Green leaves. Blue sky opens wide over the village path. She whispers each color like a small spell as she walks.

By the time she reaches the gate, the world looks bright and full of names she knows.

Every color has a place in the village evening, and Su names them all with joy.
