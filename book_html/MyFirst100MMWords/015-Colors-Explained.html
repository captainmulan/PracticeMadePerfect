Grandmother's Painting

After supper Grandmother opened a tin of paints. Together they mixed Red and White to make Pink lotus petals and Blue with Yellow for Green leaves. Su painted the Gold pagoda roof while Grandmother added a Black line for the gate. When the painting dried, Su labeled each color in English and tapped the Myanmar button beside every word. The picture hung in the house like a rainbow that never faded.
