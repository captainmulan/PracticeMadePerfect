Mangoes at the Market

Su counted mangoes at the busy market with Mother. One, two, three yellow mangoes glowed in a basket. Four more waited for Father, and five for Grandmother. The seller smiled when Su paid six coins carefully, counted again on her fingers, and said thank you with a bright polite voice.

Ten Coins on the Mat

After dinner Father spread a woven mat and laid ten shiny coins in a neat line. "Count with your eyes and your voice — do not rush," he said. Su pointed slowly from one to ten. Baby clapped at five. Grandmother checked nine and ten. Zero mistakes! Father nodded proudly and Grandmother gave Su a sweet mango.

Counting with Love

That night on the porch Su counted stars, then family smiles — Mother, Father, Grandmother, Grandfather, Sister, Baby, Aunt, Uncle, Cousin, and herself. Numbers felt like warm faces she could hold in her hands. She whispered thank you and fell asleep happy under the cool Myanmar night.
