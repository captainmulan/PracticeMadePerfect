A Busy Festival Day

The festival was loud and bright, and Su felt Happy one moment and a little Tired the next. When she lost her ribbon she turned Sad until her brother found it and she felt Grateful. A surprise firework made her Excited, then the crowd pushed close and she felt Nervous. Naming feelings helped her breathe and choose what to do next.

Feelings Like Visitors

Grandmother said feelings visit like guests — some stay long, some pass quickly. Su was Angry when her toy broke, then Calm after mending it with tape. She felt Lonely when cousin left, then Loved when mother hugged her tight. She even felt Shy meeting a new teacher and Brave when she said hello anyway.

Thank You Under the Lights

Under festival lights Su felt Proud sharing her new Myanmar words. A wrong answer made her Embarrassed, but her friend whispered, "Try again," and Hope returned. At bedtime she felt Peaceful listening to crickets. Su thanked each feeling for teaching her heart something true — joy, fear, love, and all the colors in between.
