A Busy Festival Day

The festival was loud and bright, and Su felt Happy one moment and a little Tired the next. When she lost her ribbon she turned Sad until her brother found it and she felt Grateful. A surprise firework made her Excited, then the crowd pushed close and she felt Nervous. Naming feelings helped her breathe and choose what to do next.
