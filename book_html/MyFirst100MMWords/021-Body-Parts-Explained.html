Dance Class Begins

In the school hall Su stretched her Arms and wiggled her Fingers to warm up. The teacher asked everyone to tap their Feet in time and lift their Head high. Su touched her Nose, her Ears, and her Eyes in the mirror and giggled at her reflection. Learning body words, she discovered, was like learning the map of herself.

Respect for the Body

Grandmother told Su to wash her Hands before meals and brush her Teeth after sweet mango. When Su scraped her Knee on the step, her mother cleaned the cut and kissed her Leg gently. Her brother pinched his Lip making faces until Su reminded him to be kind to his own face. Every part deserved care — that was the quiet lesson of the day.

Show Day Smiles

On show day Su stood tall, Shoulders back, Heart pounding with joy. She sang a song that used every body word she knew — from Hair to Toes. The audience clapped when she bowed and touched her Chest to say thank you. Afterward her teacher said the best performance comes from a body that listens and a voice that shares. Su believed her.
