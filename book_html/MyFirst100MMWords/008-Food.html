Sharing with the Monks

At sunrise, Kyaw waited by the lane with a bowl of rice and fresh bananas. Monks walked barefoot with empty bowls, and Kyaw carefully spooned rice into each one. He poured water from a clay pot and bowed with both hands. Mother watched from the doorway and smiled. "In Myanmar, we share food with monks before we eat our fill," she said.

Morning at the Tea Shop

Kyaw followed Grandfather to the neighborhood tea shop. Old friends sat around low tables, laughing over sweet milk tea and warm bread. One man passed around lahpet thoke while another cracked a soft-boiled egg into his soup. The shop smelled of tea and kindness. "Food brings neighbors together," Grandfather said, pouring Kyaw a tiny cup.

One More Bowl

A thin dog waited outside the gate. Kyaw saved bread and fish from his plate and filled a bowl with water. The dog wagged its tail and ate slowly. That night Kyaw fell asleep happy, still tasting mango and milk, knowing full bellies can choose to be kind.
