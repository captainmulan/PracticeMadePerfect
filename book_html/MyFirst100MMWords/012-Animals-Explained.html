Friends Under the Tree

Under the big tamarind tree Su watched an Elephant sway at the edge of the field while a Cat slept in a patch of sun. A Dog chased a Butterfly until a Bird scolded them from above. Grandmother said every creature has a job — the Buffalo in the mud, the Cow in the pasture, the Pig near the fence. Su named each friend aloud and the tree seemed to listen.

The Rainy Day

Rain drummed on the roof and the Fish pond swelled with rings. A Frog sang from the steps while a Snake hid among the reeds — Grandfather warned Su to look, not touch. Inside, the Cat curled on her lap and the Dog shook water from his fur. Through the window Su saw a Horse waiting patiently and a Monkey steal a Mango from the stall. The storm passed, and every animal word felt alive.

A Lesson for the Village

When the sky cleared, the village teacher brought children to the pond. They fed the Fish, counted the Ducks, and drew the Tiger from a story scroll — not a real one, Su was glad! An Ox pulled a cart past the school while a Rat peeked from a rice sack and scurried away. Su practiced each animal name as a promise to respect life around her home.
