Rain Under the House

Monsoon rain tapped the Roof while Su read in her Room. Water ran from the Door step into the Garden where flowers bent low. Inside, the Kitchen smelled of ginger and the Bathroom steam fogged the mirror. Su listed each place in the house — living room, bedroom, stairs — and felt safe under one roof.

Every Room Helps

Su helped sweep the Floor and fold blankets on the Bed in the guest Room. Her father fixed a Window that stuck and oiled the Gate hinge. Mother arranged chairs in the Hall for visitors. Even the small Storage closet had a job — holding rice and spare candles. A home, Su learned, is a team of rooms working together.

Guests at the Door

When neighbors arrived, Su opened the Door wide and offered Water in the Kitchen. Guests sat in the Living room while rain whispered on the Roof. Later she showed her cousin the Garden path and the Room where baby napped. Saying home words in Myanmar made the house feel bigger — as if every wall could welcome a friend.
