Mangoes at the Market

Su counted One basket, then Two, then Three mangoes in a row. The seller called Four and Five until Ten fruit piled high. Mother gave her Six coins and asked her to bring back Seven limes. Su practiced numbers while bargaining politely, because in Myanmar markets, good manners matter as much as good math.

Ten Coins on the Mat

At home Su laid coins on a mat — One for rice, Two for tea, Three for soap. Her father showed Four fingers, then Eight, teaching her to double without fear. Grandmother quizzed her from One to Nine while baby clapped at Ten. When Su missed Seven she laughed and tried again. Numbers became friends she could call by name.
