Walk to School

Su walks to School with her Bag on her shoulder. Her Friend walks beside her along the dusty path. They wave to the Teacher at the gate with bright smiles. Su opens her Book at her desk and takes out a Pencil. A new day of learning begins with hope.
Su walks to School with her Bag on her shoulder in the cool morning. Her Friend walks beside her along the dusty path and shares a joke. They wave to the Teacher at the gate with bright smiles. Inside, Su opens her Book at her desk and takes out a Pencil. The Class grows quiet when the lesson starts. Su listens carefully and feels ready. A new day of learning begins with hope and courage.

Learning One Line

Su raises her Hand to answer a question in class. The Teacher nods with a warm smile. Su shares her Pencil with a friend who forgot one. She writes one careful line in her Book. School words open doors in her growing mind.
Su raises her Hand to answer a question in class and speaks clearly. The Teacher nods with a warm smile and writes the word on the board. Su shares her Pencil with a friend who forgot one at home. She writes one careful line in her Book and checks it twice. Her Friend whispers well done. Su feels brave enough to try again. School words open doors in her growing mind every single day.

Reading Aloud

Su reads aloud to the Class with a steady voice. At first she feels shy, then her words grow strong. Her Friend claps the loudest when she finishes. The Teacher praises her clear reading. Su is proud to belong at School.
Su reads aloud to the Class with a steady voice on Friday morning. At first she feels shy, then her words grow strong and clear. She holds her Book with both hands and looks up between lines. Her Friend claps the loudest when she finishes the page. The Teacher praises her clear reading and kind courage. Classmates smile from every desk. Su is proud to belong at School and to share what she has learned.
