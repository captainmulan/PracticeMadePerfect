Walk to School

Su walked to School with her Bag bouncing and her Friend beside her. They passed the Teacher's garden and waved to the Principal at the gate. Inside the Classroom, desks waited in neat rows and the Library smelled of old paper. Su hung her coat and whispered hello to every part of the place where she learned to grow.

Reading Aloud

Before going home Su stood before the class to read aloud. Her voice shook at first, then grew steady like the school bell. The Teacher wrote a star beside her name on the Board and her Friend clapped loudest. Walking out, Su looked back at the Classroom windows glowing gold. She was proud to belong to this school family.
