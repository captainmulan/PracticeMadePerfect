Sharing with the Monks

Mother scoops Rice into clean bowls before sunrise. Father fills cups with cool Water for the morning offering. Neighbors share Fish and ripe Mango on a long mat. Su carries a small bowl with careful hands. She learns that sharing food is a kind and happy way to start the day.
Mother scoops Rice into clean bowls before sunrise. Father fills cups with cool Water for the morning offering. Neighbors share Fish and ripe Mango on a long mat outside the house. Aunt brings Soup while Uncle carries fresh Bread. Su carries a small bowl with careful hands and walks beside Mother. The monks smile and bless the family. Su learns that sharing food is a kind and happy way to start the day in her village.

Morning at the Tea Shop

Su passes the tea shop on the way to school. Uncle buys warm Bread and a boiled Egg for breakfast. Su sips sweet Tea and watches the busy street. Noodle steam rises like little clouds above the pots. She says thank you, then walks on with a full and cheerful heart.
Su passes the tea shop on the way to school with her bag on her shoulder. Uncle buys warm Bread and a boiled Egg for breakfast. Su sips sweet Tea and watches the busy street wake up. Noodle steam rises like little clouds above the pots. A seller offers Fruit, and Father chooses a yellow Mango. Su says thank you for the kind meal. She walks on toward school with a full and cheerful heart.

The family gathers for dinner around the low table. Mother adds hot Soup to every bowl. Father shares Fish and asks Su to pour Water. She also pours Tea for Grandmother and Grandfather. One more bowl of Rice makes the meal feel complete and shared by all.
