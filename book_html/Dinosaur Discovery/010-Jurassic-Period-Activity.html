Picture · Green Cathedral

📖 Green Cathedral

Kai walked beneath tree ferns taller than houses. "Welcome to the Jurassic," Dr. Mara smiled. Humidity clung to his skin. The air smelled of sap and river mud. This was the age when dinosaurs grew bold — and big.
💡 Explanation: The Jurassic had warm climates and rising seas. Conifers, cycads, and ferns dominated forests. More moisture and plant food supported larger plant-eating dinosaurs.

Picture · Neck Above the Clouds

📖 Neck Above the Clouds

Kai craned his head until his hat fell off. A Brachiosaurus model loomed in the museum atrium — heart the size of a car, neck like a bridge. "Plant-eaters needed reach," Dr. Mara said. "Why chew low when the salad bar is upstairs?"
💡 Explanation: Sauropods like Brachiosaurus and Diplodocus were the largest land animals ever. Long necks let them browse high vegetation without moving their massive bodies often.

Picture · Plates in the Sun

📖 Plates in the Sun

Stegosaurus marched through Kai's notebook, back plates glowing in his crayon sunset. "Were they armor, fans, or billboards?" he asked. Dr. Mara grinned. "Science still argues. Maybe all three."
💡 Explanation: Stegosaurus had bony plates and spiked tail (thagomizer). Plates may have regulated body heat or signaled to others. It was a slow, well-defended herbivore.
