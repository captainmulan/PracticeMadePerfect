Learn · Your hidden frame

📖 Your hidden frame

Maya stands tall and wiggles her fingers. Under her skin, 206 bones form a frame that holds her up — like the poles inside a tent.
💡 Explanation: Bones give your body shape and support. Adults have 206 bones; babies start with more that fuse as they grow.
Learn · Living and growing

📖 Living and growing

Maya breaks a small fall with her hands and is glad nothing cracks. Her bones aren't dead sticks — they're alive, growing, and fixing small cracks.
💡 Explanation: Bones contain living cells. They store minerals like calcium and make new blood cells inside bone marrow.
Learn · Joints that bend

📖 Joints that bend

Maya's elbow bends but her upper arm doesn't flop. Joints — where bones meet — let some parts hinge, swivel, or glide.
💡 Explanation: Hinge joints (like elbows and knees) bend one way. Ball-and-socket joints (like hips and shoulders) rotate more freely.
