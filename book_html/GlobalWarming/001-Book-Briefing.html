📖 About This Book

Welcome to Global Warming! Join Maya and Professor Leaf on an eco adventure across our changing planet. You'll learn why Earth is warming, how greenhouse gases work, and what we can do to build a greener future.

👧 Maya   🍃 Professor Leaf

Maya is a curious young explorer who asks big questions. Professor Leaf is a friendly climate scientist with a leaf-badge lab coat — she guides Maya through forests, ice caps, oceans, and clean-energy labs!

💬 Author's Message

"Hello, young eco hero! I'm Jimmy Cooper, and I created this book to help you understand our amazing planet. From greenhouse gases to melting ice, from clean energy to wildlife — every chapter is a step toward protecting Earth. Explore, play the games, and become a climate champion!"

🎯 What You'll Learn

  • How Earth's climate and weather work
  • The greenhouse effect and carbon dioxide
  • Fossil fuels and renewable energy
  • Melting ice, rising seas, and wildlife at risk
  • How to build a green future together

🎮 How to Use This Book

  • 📖 Read briefing and index, then choose your eco hero
  • 🌱 Play the Eco Garden intro, then explore each topic
  • 🎯 Each topic: activity + explained + quiz
  • 🏆 Finish with conclusion, overall quiz, and Eco Sort game

🌍 Why Climate Matters

Earth's climate affects every living thing — from butterflies in your garden to polar bears on ice. When we understand how the planet works, we can make smarter choices to keep it healthy for generations to come.
