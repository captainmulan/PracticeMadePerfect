Want to Play?

Story

Under the wide tree a girl asks, Want to play? Mudra answers yes and joins the fun.

After school children gather under a wide tree. Want to play? a girl asks. Mudra smiles and answers yes. They run and jump until their cheeks glow in the afternoon light.

Catch Me

Story

Let's go! Catch me! Mudra learns run, jump, and hide with her new friends.

Let's go! shouts a boy. Catch me! Mudra practices yes, no, run, jump, and hide. When she feels shy, Friend holds her hand and says she can play with them.

Not Far Anymore

Story

At sunset Grandma finds Mudra under the tree. You made friends. The village feels closer.

At sunset Grandma finds Mudra under the tree. You made friends, she says. Mudra waves goodbye. For the first time the village does not feel far from her heart.
