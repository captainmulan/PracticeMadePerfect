Hungry Chickens

Uncle calls that the chickens are hungry. Mudra carries grain past the cow and goat.

Uncle calls from the farm path. The chickens are hungry! Mudra carries a small bowl of grain. Chickens peck. A cow watches. A goat bleats near the hay while a dog wags its tail.

Soft Hands

Uncle teaches soft hands. Mudra feeds animals and collects warm eggs into a basket.

Eggs for Dinner

Grandma claps for Mudra's farm eggs. Helping the farm feels like loving the village.

Grandma claps when Mudra returns with the basket. Tonight we cook with your farm eggs. Mudra feels tall inside. Helping the farm is another way to love her village family.
