⛰️ Landforms Recap

Mountains rise high above the clouds. Valleys nestle between peaks. Deserts stretch hot and dry, while rivers carve paths through the land. Plains offer wide open spaces for farms and cities. These landforms appear on every continent and tell the story of how Earth changes over time.

Plate tectonics — the slow movement of Earth's crust — builds mountains, causes earthquakes, and moves continents. The Himalayas grow taller each year because India pushes into Asia!

🗺️ Why Geography Matters

Understanding continents and landforms helps us:

  • Appreciate different cultures and ways of life
  • Understand weather, climate, and where animals live
  • Protect forests, rivers, and wildlife habitats
  • Travel wisely and respect the places we visit
  • See how all people share one beautiful planet
