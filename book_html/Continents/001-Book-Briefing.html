📖 About This Book

Continents Adventure takes you around the world — from African savannas to Antarctic ice, Asian mountains to Australian reefs.

Each chapter has painted pictures, a short story with Maya, tap-to-hear words, a mini-game, and a quiz. Three big games bookend the journey!

🎮 Three Hero Games

Start · Page 4 🗺️ World Map Maker Stamp continents, animals & landmarks onto your own world map.
Mid-book · 019b 🧭 Continent Trek Jump hazards and collect all seven continent icons!
Finale · Page 34 🌍 Globe Rush Lane-run the globe — grab treasures, dodge desert storms!
