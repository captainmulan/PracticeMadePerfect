Looking Down at Myanmar

From the airplane Mudra sees rice fields and pagodas. She whispers that today is her first holiday in Myanmar.

From the airplane Mudra sees green rice fields and golden pagodas. She whispers that today is her first holiday in Myanmar. Hope and a little worry sit together in her heart as the plane flies lower toward home.

Grandma's Hug

Grandma waves at the gate. Mudra runs into her hug. Welcome home, Grandma says with shining eyes.

Grandma waves both hands at the airport gate. Mudra runs into her soft hug. The air smells like rain and flowers. Welcome home, Grandma says, holding Mudra's small suitcase with shining eyes.

Practice Mingalarpar

Grandma teaches Mingalarpar, thank you, and come. Kind words, she says, open every door.

Grandma teaches Mingalarpar carefully. Mudra practices hello, thank you, and come. Kind words open every door in our village, Grandma says, and Mudra repeats each sound until it feels friendly in her mouth.
