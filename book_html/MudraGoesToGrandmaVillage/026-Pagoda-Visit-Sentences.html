Shoes by the Steps

Please remove your shoes, Grandma whispers. Mudra places them neatly and steps softly.

On a cool morning they walk to the shining pagoda. Please remove your shoes, Grandma whispers. Mudra places them neatly beside Grandma's pair and steps softly onto the cool stone.
