🍜 Tea Shop Morning · Sentences

Practice session · 3 picture story blocks

Hand in Hand

Grandma and Mudra walk to the tea shop. The seller smiles. The air smells of bread and tea.

After breakfast Grandma takes Mudra's hand. Let's buy tea. They walk past red flowers to a small shop. The seller smiles. Warm bread and milky tea perfume the morning air.

Tea, Please

Mudra says Mingalarpar and tea, please. Grandma asks how much. Mudra says thank you.

Mudra greets the seller with Mingalarpar and asks for tea, please. Two cups and bread arrive. Grandma asks how much. Coins click softly. Mudra remembers to say thank you with a bright voice.

Steady the Cup

Mudra sits with Aunt. She practices cup and plate. Grandma steadies her hand when tea tips.

Mudra sits with Aunt and a neighbor. She practices cup, plate, tea, and bread. When the cup tips, Grandma steadies her hand and laughs. Slow and careful. Mudra feels proud to share the table.
