🕯️ Chapter 19 · The Lantern Evening

19.1

Glowing Lights

Mudra walked with a lantern, holding the handle with both hands. The little flame trembled and then settled. All the children carried lanterns home, their faces warm and flushed with excitement. The path looked like a string of tiny suns.

At home, they placed their lanterns on the porch and watched as the night held each small light like a promise. Mudra whispered a wish and kept it safe in her pocket.
