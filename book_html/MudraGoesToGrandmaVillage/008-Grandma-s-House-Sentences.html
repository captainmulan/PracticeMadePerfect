The Wooden House

Mother cooks rice. Father sweeps. Sister hangs Mudra's dress. Baby claps when Mudra walks in.

Grandma's wooden house stands on stilts beside banana trees. Mother cooks rice. Father sweeps the yard. Sister hangs Mudra's dress by the window. Baby claps the moment Mudra walks through the door.

Say Every Name

Mudra learns Mother, Father, Sister, Brother, Aunt, Uncle, Cousin, and Baby. I am Mudra, she says.

This is your family, Grandma says. Mudra learns Mother, Father, Sister, Brother, Aunt, Uncle, Cousin, and Baby. She taps her chest and says, I am Mudra. Everyone laughs with kind, warm faces.

Grandfather First

Mudra greets Grandfather first with Mingalarpar. He welcomes the little traveler under their roof.

Before dinner Grandma whispers to greet Grandfather first. Mudra bows a little and says Mingalarpar, Grandfather. He calls her little traveler and promises she will sleep safely under their roof tonight.
