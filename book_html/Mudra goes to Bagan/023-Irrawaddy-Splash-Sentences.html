Silver Road

Story

The Irrawaddy moves like a wide silver road. Boats bob. Birds argue politely.

Auntie Hla takes them to the Irrawaddy. The river moves like a wide silver road. Boats bob. Birds argue politely in bird language after the big balloon feelings.

Press sentences to hear
The Irrawaddy river is wide.
ဧရာဝတီမြစ်က ကျယ်တယ်။
A small boat bobs on water.
လှေလေးက ရေပေါ်မှာ ယိမ်းတယ်။
Mudra finds a new friend.
မုဒြာက သူငယ်ချင်းအသစ် ရတယ်။
The children want to play.
ကလေးတွေ ကစားချင်တယ်။

Want to Play?

Story

A girl with a bright ribbon asks, Want to play? Mudra answers yes!

Local kids skip stones. A girl with a bright ribbon jogs over. Want to play? Mudra's answer comes easier than before. Yes!

Press sentences to hear
Mudra answers yes with joy.
မုဒြာက ပျော်ပျော် ဟုတ်ကဲ့ လို့ ဖြေတယ်။
No is also a useful word.
မဟုတ်ပါ ဆိုတာလည်း အသုံးဝင်တယ်။
Come here and share mango.
ဒီကိုလာပါ သရက်သီး မျှစားကြမယ်။
They jump on the sand.
သူတို့ သဲပေါ်မှာ ခုန်တယ်။

Friendship Shower

Story

Ko Ko's mighty stone skip becomes a friendship shower. Everyone laughs with sandy toes.

Ko Ko tries a mighty stone skip and creates one magnificent splash that waters everyone's ankles. Sorry! Friendship shower! the ribbon girl yells, laughing. Mudra shares mango pieces like treasure.

Press sentences to hear
Sandy toes feel funny.
သဲစွဲခြေချောင်းက ရယ်စရာကောင်းတယ်။
Friends share snacks kindly.
သူငယ်ချင်းတွေ အဆာပြေကို ကြင်နာစွာ မျှတယ်။
