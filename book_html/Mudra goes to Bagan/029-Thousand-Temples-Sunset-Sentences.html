Biscuit-Gold Sky

Story

Sunset turns the plain orange, pink, then biscuit-gold. Temples become silhouettes.

On the last evening Auntie Hla leads them to a sunset viewpoint. The plain turns orange, then pink, then a color Mudra invents: biscuit-gold. Temples become silhouettes.

Happy Bagan

Story

Auntie Hla invents Happy Bagan. Mudra answers thank you, Bagan, and laughs.

This is the peak — bigger somehow than balloon roar or river splash. Happy Bagan, Auntie Hla invents. Mudra laughs and answers, Thank you, Bagan.

See You in Words

Story

Grandma says together is the point. Mudra writes goodbye is see you in temples and words.

Grandma cups Mudra's face. Now you can speak Myanmar with Bagan too. Not perfectly, Mudra says. Perfectly is not the point. Together is the point. Mudra writes: Goodbye is see you in temples and words.
