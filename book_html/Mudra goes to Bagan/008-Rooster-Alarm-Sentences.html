Brick House Welcome

Story

Auntie Hla's brick guest house smells of tea and soap. She laughs like a bouncing radio.

Auntie Hla's brick guest house smells of tea and laundry soap. She has round cheeks and a bouncing laugh. She shows Mudra a mosquito-net bed that looks like a princess tent.

Rooster Emergency

Story

A rooster screams before dawn. Mudra asks if it is an emergency. Grandma says it is Bagan time.

A rooster screams like a tiny fire truck before dawn. Mudra sits up so fast her M clip almost flies away. Is it an emergency? Grandma groans. It is Bagan time.

I Am Mudra

Story

At breakfast Mudra learns house words and says I am Mudra. Auntie Hla grins and pours more tea.

At breakfast Mudra learns bed, water, breakfast, and thank you. She taps her chest and says I am Mudra. Auntie Hla bows with a grin and claims she is almost the rooster's boss.
