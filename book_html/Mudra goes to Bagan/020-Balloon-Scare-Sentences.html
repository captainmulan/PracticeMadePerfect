Peanut Courage

Story

Balloon morning arrives before the rooster. Mudra's courage feels the size of a peanut.

Long before the rooster, Grandma wakes Mudra for balloon morning. Giant colorful onions fill with fire-breath. Mudra's courage suddenly feels the size of a peanut. She holds Grandma's skirt like a seatbelt.

Brave Means Looking

Story

Brave does not mean no fear, Grandma says. Brave means looking anyway.

We watch from the ground first, Grandma promises. Burners roar. A balloon lifts — slow, huge, impossible. Brave does not mean no fear, Grandma says. Brave means looking anyway.

Wow!

Story

Pink light paints every brick. Mudra forgets her fear and shouts wow. Ko Ko sneezes an amazed sneeze.

Pink light paints every brick. Mudra forgets to grip the skirt. Wow, she breathes, then louder, Wow! Ko Ko tries a deep movie wow and sneezes instead. Even the sneeze sounds amazed.
