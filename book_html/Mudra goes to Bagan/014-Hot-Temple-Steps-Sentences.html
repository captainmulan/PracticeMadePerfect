Brick Mountain

Story

The first temple rises like a brick mountain. Mudra's neck tilts until her M clip points at the sky.

The first big temple rises like a brick mountain with a golden tip. Mudra's neck tilts until her M clip points at the sky. Ko Ko whispers that his legs are small. Grandma says climb with small brave steps.

Clown Sandals

Story

Please remove your shoes. Ko Ko swaps left and right sandals and hops like a clown.

Window Over Bagan

Story

From a high window Mudra sees temples like clay toys for giants. Hot steps, cool heart.

Inside, they offer flowers and light a candle. From a high window Bagan spreads out — temples like clay toys for giants. Mudra writes: Hot steps. Cool heart. Shoes waiting downstairs like loyal dogs.
