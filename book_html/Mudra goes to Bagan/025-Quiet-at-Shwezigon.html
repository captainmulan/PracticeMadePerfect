On a cool evening they walked to Shwezigon. The pagoda shone like a held breath of gold. Mudra felt the week's jokes settle into something deeper — not sad, just big.

"Please remove your shoes," Grandma whispered. Mudra placed them neatly. No clown sandals tonight. Ko Ko was quiet too, which was a historical event.
