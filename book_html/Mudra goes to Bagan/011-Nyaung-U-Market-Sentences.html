Please, This One

Story

Mudra points to a sweet snack and says I want this one, please. Then she remembers thank you.

Mudra points to a round sweet snack. I want this one. Please. Coins click. Thank you, she adds. The seller gives her an extra smile for free.

Hat Bonk

Story

Ko Ko wears a hat so big he walks into mangoes with a soft bonk. He announces he is fine.

Ko Ko tries on a sun hat that covers his eyes completely. He walks into a soft mango pile with a soft bonk. I am fine, he announces from under the hat. The mangoes are also fine. Mostly.
