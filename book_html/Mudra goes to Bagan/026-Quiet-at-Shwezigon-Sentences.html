Held Breath of Gold

Story

Shwezigon shines like a held breath of gold. The week's jokes settle into something deeper.

On a cool evening they walk to Shwezigon. The pagoda shines like a held breath of gold. Mudra feels the week's jokes settle into something deeper — not sad, just big.

Story
