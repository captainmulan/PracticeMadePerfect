Jingle Wheels

Story

Ko Ko rents jingling bicycles. Mudra adjusts her M clip for aerodynamic bravery.

Cousin Ko Ko rents two bicycles that jingle like happy goats. I am the captain, he announces. Mudra adjusts her M clip for aerodynamic bravery. Grandma follows on a bike that refuses to hurry.

Goat Officer

Story

A goat steps into the road like a traffic officer. Mudra says hello, goat. The goat moves.

A goat steps into the road like a furry traffic officer. Ko Ko squeaks. Mudra brakes. Hello, goat, she says. The goat chews thoughtfully, then moves. Victory.

Bagan's Bounce

Story

Auntie Hla says Mudra belongs on these paths. Belonging can be wheels, dust, and laughter.

At a shady temple they drink water. Auntie Hla arrives by e-bike like a queen of breeze. You belong on these paths, she says. Mudra feels it — belonging can be wheels, dust, laughter, and knowing when to stop for goats.
